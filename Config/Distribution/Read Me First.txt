University Portal
=================

Your Moodle and CMS (cms.sic.saarland) in one Mac app: deadlines, announcements,
materials with a built-in PDF viewer, and your week's schedule. Everything stays on
your Mac; nothing is sent anywhere except to Moodle and CMS themselves, and to
ChatGPT when you use Ask.

Needs: any Mac (Apple silicon or Intel) with macOS 15 or newer, and your own
Saarland University Moodle account (CMS is optional).


1. Install
----------
Drag "University Portal" onto the Applications folder in this window.


2. Open it the first time
-------------------------
The app isn't from the App Store or a registered Apple developer, so macOS blocks
it the first time. This is expected. To allow it once:

  1. Open University Portal from Applications. macOS says it can't be opened. Click "Done".
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the message about "University Portal" and click "Open Anyway".
  4. Confirm with your Mac password. From then on it opens normally.

(Alternative in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/University Portal.app")


3. Connect Moodle
-----------------
  1. Open Settings (Cmd+,) and click "Connect in Browser…".
  2. Moodle opens in your browser; sign in with your university account if asked.
  3. When the browser asks to open "University Portal", allow it.

If the browser never asks (for example because the official Moodle app is installed and
takes the link), use "Paste the redirect instead" in Settings and follow the steps there.

Your university password is never stored; only the Moodle app token is, in your Keychain.


4. Add CMS courses (optional)
-----------------------------
In Settings > CMS:
  - "Choose Courses…" adds a course's news and calendar (no login needed).
  - Sign in with your CMS username and password to also get materials. They are saved in
    your Keychain; after a wrong password the app stops trying until you sign in again.


5. Ask your materials (optional, needs ChatGPT Plus or Pro)
----------------------------------------------------------
  1. Open Ask in the sidebar and click "Continue with ChatGPT". Sign in in your browser
     and allow University Portal to use your ChatGPT plan.
  2. Click "Index now" once; new materials are indexed automatically afterwards.
  3. Ask a question. Answers cite your PDFs; click [1] to open the page. Try
     "make flashcards for ..." or "visualize ...".

Only your question and the matching excerpts are sent to OpenAI, never whole files.
Usage counts against your ChatGPT plan (Manage usage in Ask).


Good to know
------------
  - When macOS asks whether University Portal may use items in your Keychain, choose
    "Always Allow".
  - The app syncs while it's running, also with the window closed (menu bar icon).
    Settings > General > "Open at login" keeps it running.
  - Right-click an event in Schedule to hide a category, e.g. tutorials that aren't yours.
