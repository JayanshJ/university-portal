## A native Mac design
University Portal now looks and works like a Mac app, and follows your Mac's light or dark appearance.

- **Sidebar and toolbar** like Mail and Notes, with unread counts, course colours, and sync status at the bottom.
- **Today, Deadlines and course pages** are clean lists like Reminders. Each deadline has a completion circle in its course colour, and later deadlines are grouped by month.
- **Announcements** work like Mail: unread dots, previews, and the message on the right.
- **Materials** look like Finder, with file icons, folding sections, and search in the toolbar.
- **Schedule** looks like Calendar's week view, with ‹ Today › in the toolbar.
- **Ask** answers follow light and dark mode and use your system accent colour.

After this update, macOS asks once for Keychain access. Choose **Always Allow**.
