## Ask matches the rest of the app
- Questions appear as bubbles, like in Messages, and answers as plain text in the system font, in both light and dark mode.
- Copy and Regenerate sit under each answer.
- Sources, flashcards and visualizations have a cleaner, native look.
- Formatting is unchanged: math, tables, code, flashcards, visualizations, and citations that open the PDF at the right page.

After this update, macOS asks once for Keychain access. Choose **Always Allow**.
