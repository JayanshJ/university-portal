## Hand in assignments
**Submit…** on any Moodle assignment opens a sheet with the task, its files, what you've already handed in, and Moodle's limits. Add files by picking or dragging them in, write online text if the assignment takes it, then **Save Draft** or **Submit for Grading** (it asks for confirmation first).

## Everything from Moodle, in the app
- **Forums**: every discussion forum, each thread with all its replies. Announcements now include replies too.
- **Messages**: your Moodle direct messages.
- **Grades**: each course's gradebook with feedback. New grades show on Today.
- **Course Page**: course descriptions, section texts and notes on each course.
- **More deadlines**: closing dates of feedback forms, quizzes and other activities.
- **More files**: files attached to assignments and forum posts appear in Materials.

## Sessions and presentations from course texts
If you're signed in to ChatGPT, the app reads your Moodle course descriptions and announcements for weekly sessions, exams and presentations, including ones stated only in words like "next week's pitch session". It adds them to Today and Schedule with their room, a **Mandatory** tag where the course says so, and where they were mentioned.

## Open any file
Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Keynote, images, video and more now open inside the app.

After this update, macOS asks once for Keychain access. Choose **Always Allow**.
